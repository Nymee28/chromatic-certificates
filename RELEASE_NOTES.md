# v0.1.0 — First public release (2026-07-29)

Scope of this release:

- Theorems 1-2 (G(q,3), ZJ families): statements and evidence as consolidated in
  docs/fr/recueil-theoremes.md, with the supporting toolchain and registries.
- Theorem 3 (G_CH(q,4), modulus 4): complete chain — finite presentation
  (6,487 labels, two parity strata V_even/V_odd, denominators | 16), exact
  solutions and blind-prediction verdicts for q = 3...12, closure lemma
  (docs/fr/lemme-C-mod4.md) with its open verification list [R].
- Collapse Conjecture: statement, structural argument (diameter <= 6 / Moore),
  and the 170-verdict adversarial campaign (random hunts n = 14-17,
  edge-critical cores, densified instances).
- Conditional NC^2 corollary with its stage-by-stage certification.

Known gaps, stated plainly: the closure lemma carries an explicit [R] list;
the Collapse Conjecture is a conjecture; docs are French-first (English
statements in docs/en/results.md, full translations in progress). Further
program documents (finite-presentation concept, P-vs-NC cartography,
continuous-margin instrumentation) will follow in v0.2.
